GLOSSARY OF METRICS
Normalized Mean Cluster Size: The mass-weighted average size of finite obstacle clusters normalized by the total voxel volume. Excludes the largest cluster.
Mean Aspect Ratio: Bounding box geometric stretch (Length / Width) of the clusters weighted by their area.
Moran's I: Global spatial autocorrelation. +1.0 = highly clustered, 0.0 = random noise.
Fractal Dimension: Measures the complexity and space-filling capacity of the fibrosis boundaries.
Betti 0: Number of connected healthy tissue components.
Betti 1: Number of 1D healthy loops (fibrotic islands) inside the healthy tissue.
Betti 2 (3D only): Number of enclosed voids (fully enclosed fibrotic pockets) in the healthy tissue.
Topological Conduction Efficiency (TCE): The structural speed limit of the mesh (Linear Length / Average Percolating Path Length).
Shortest Percolating Path: The absolute minimal steps required to cross the domain X-axis.
